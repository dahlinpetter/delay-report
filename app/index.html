About

Train data from Trafiklab.se (CC-BY 4.0) and its KoDa archive. Backtrack is an independent service and is not affiliated with Skånetrafiken, Öresundståg or DSB.

Privacy

Backtrack has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. Your commute, trip log and claims are stored only on this phone.

If you turn on notifications, Backtrack's server stores your phone's push address and the stations and times it needs for your reminders. Nothing else.

  • Night-out reminders are deleted an hour after the train has left.
  • Without the evening check, everything is deleted within a few hours of your last reminder.
  • With the evening check, morning alert or ticket reminder on, your data is kept while you use Backtrack and deleted after 30 days without opening the app.
  • "Turn off and delete my data" deletes everything immediately.

The server runs on Cloudflare and the app is hosted on GitHub, which process data on Backtrack's behalf. Questions or requests: contact details will be added here.
