Help

About, privacy and data

Train data

Live and historical train data from Trafiklab.se (CC-BY 4.0), including its KoDa archive.

Independent service

Backtrack is not affiliated with Skånetrafiken, Öresundståg or DSB.

Compensation

Backtrack shows delays from public traffic data. Only claim compensation for trips you actually made with a valid ticket. Amounts are estimates, and Skånetrafiken assesses every claim. A 30-day ticket is valued at 1/20 of its price per trip. Compensation taken as a discount code is shown with a 50% bonus; check Skånetrafiken's current terms.

Privacy

Backtrack has no accounts. Your commute, ticket details, trips and claims are stored only on this phone.

If you add your details for claim forms (name, personnummer, address, phone, email), they are stored only on this phone and used to fill in the PDF you create. They are never sent to Backtrack. Your signature is used for that one PDF and not saved.

If you turn on notifications, Backtrack's server stores your phone's push address and the stations and times it needs for your reminders. Nothing else.

  • Night-out reminders are deleted an hour after the train has left.
  • Without the morning alert, evening check or ticket reminder, everything is deleted within a few hours of your last reminder.
  • With them on, your data is kept while you use Backtrack and deleted after 30 days without opening the app.
  • "Turn off and delete my data" deletes everything immediately.

The server runs on Cloudflare and the app is hosted on GitHub, which process data on Backtrack's behalf. Questions or requests: contact details will be added here.
